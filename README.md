# The Data Between Us

**[Read the novel →](https://ashishpatelengineering.github.io/the_data_between_us/)**

A novel about data engineering, told as a story.

Meera runs a small food delivery company in Bengaluru. One Sunday she cannot answer a simple question about her own business, because her spreadsheet spells one shop five different ways. Over the next two years, with the help of her oldest friend Mohan, she learns to build systems that people can trust. Somewhere along the way, the two of them stop pretending the lessons are the only reason they meet.

## What's inside

Thirty chapters, in five parts, moving from a single messy spreadsheet to a data platform serving ten cities:

- **Part One, One Kitchen** — databases, data modelling, keys, types, SQL, transactions
- **Part Two, A Bigger Kitchen** — indexing, warehouses, ETL and ELT, star schemas, slowly changing dimensions
- **Part Three, Many Kitchens** — partitioning, distributed computing, cloud storage, the lakehouse
- **Part Four, Data That Flows** — streaming, Kafka, delivery guarantees, orchestration, dbt, data quality
- **Part Five, A Grown-Up System** — governance, privacy, infrastructure as code, data for AI, schema evolution, cost

Every chapter ends with a short summary of what was learned, so the book works as a reference as well as a story.

## The dataset

Every number in the book comes from one small dataset: Zaayka's cities, kitchens, dishes, customers, riders, orders, and order items. Nothing is invented for a single example. Every query, every table, and every total traces back to the same rows, which are listed in full in the appendix.

## Who it's for

Anyone who was ever handed a spreadsheet and felt a small doubt in their chest. No prior experience with databases is assumed. Each idea is explained in plain language before it is given its technical name.
